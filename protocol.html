Review protocol

The method is described in Section 3 of the paper. This page gives only what the paper leaves out. The full protocol (version 5) is kept with the review files.

Search

  • PubMed has no abstract-only field, so its abstract run used title and abstract together.
  • Every run was stored with the string as executed, the field, the filters, the date and the number of results the source reported. The results are listed on the step 1 page.

Duplicates and venue

  • Titles matched exactly were merged only if their publication years were no more than one year apart.
  • A journal was matched to the Scimago Journal Rank by ISSN, then by title. Its best quartile was used.

Quality questions in full

  1. Are the aims clearly stated? Does the study state what it sets out to collect, model or predict?
  2. Do the measures allow the questions to be answered? Can the study connect its data to the addiction, value or disorder it set out to study?
  3. Are the data collection methods described? Does it say which data were captured, and how?
  4. Are the statistical methods justified? Does it explain and justify its statistical or AI approach?
  5. Were the basic data described? Are the sample, variables and results shown, not only summarised?
  6. Do the authors explain the consequences of problems with the validity or reliability of their measures?

Changes made during the review

  • Filter layer 2 was made more precise twice after full texts had been read and scored: the young-people rule was applied to every RQ1 study, a translated version of a scale stopped counting on that ground alone, and young people were defined to include young adults aged up to 29. Six studies read under the earlier wording failed the final one.
  • Studies in which smartphone addiction was only a predictor of another outcome were excluded, as they answer none of the questions. Three scored studies were removed on this ground.
  • The quality cut-off was 3 when the studies were scored and was set at 3.5 afterwards. This removed 14 studies that scored 3.
  • Four full texts were read before the final title and abstract decisions and are excluded at step 5. In all, 95 full texts were read.

Tools

Python for searching, duplicates, selection counts, the PRISMA diagram and these pages; OpenAlex to find each venue's ISSN; Scimago Journal Rank 2025 and CORE Conference Ranking 2023 for the venue step; Word for writing. AI tools assisted the review.
