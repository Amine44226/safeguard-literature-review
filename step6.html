Step 6. Selection per question

Studies that fail filter layer 2 are removed. 89 studies left. The step started from 694.

Filter layer 2 kept, for each question, the studies that answer it most directly. A study was kept for a question if it:

  • RQ1: studied how smartphone addiction is measured in young people, and gave a new or revised scale, a cut-off, a definition, a check against phone data, or an Arabic or Persian version;
  • RQ2: used data recorded by the phone;
  • RQ3: measured human values in young people, or followed young people over time and tested the link with a mental health problem in both directions, or collected daily or momentary data on their mental health;
  • RQ4: used machine learning or built a system.

Young people are school pupils, university students, including health-science students, and adolescents. 89 studies were kept: RQ1 24, RQ2 16, RQ3 31, RQ4 32 (a study can be kept for more than one question). 605 studies were removed.
