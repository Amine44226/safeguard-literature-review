Step 6. Selection per question

Studies that fail filter layer 2 are removed. 93 studies left. The step started from 694.

Filter layer 2 kept, for each question, the studies that answer it most directly. A study was kept for a question if it:

  • RQ1: studied how smartphone addiction is measured in young people, and gave a new or revised scale, a cut-off, a definition, or a check against phone data;
  • RQ2: used data recorded by the phone;
  • RQ3: measured human values in young people, or followed young people over time and tested the link with a mental health problem in both directions, or collected daily or momentary data on their mental health;
  • RQ4: used machine learning or built a system.

Young people are school and university students, including health-science students, adolescents, and young adults aged up to 29. A sample counts if it consists of school or university students or adolescents, or if its stated age range lies within 10 to 29. Students count at any age. If only a mean age is reported, the described population decides. The rule is judged on the abstract: a sample that the abstract does not describe does not count. In a study with several samples, one sample that meets the definition is enough.

93 studies were kept: RQ1 27, RQ2 16, RQ3 32, RQ4 32 (a study can be kept for more than one question). 601 studies were removed.
