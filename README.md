# SAFEGUARD literature review: study explorer

Supporting pages for the systematic literature review *Smartphone addiction, digital biomarkers,
psychological correlates and artificial intelligence*, part of the SAFEGUARD doctoral project
(Université Lumière Lyon 2, University of Doha for Science & Technology).

The pages hold bibliographic details, decisions and quality scores only. Abstracts and full texts are
not included; each study links to its DOI. The same data are in `data/studies.csv` and
`data/search_results.csv`.
