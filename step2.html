Step 2. Duplicates

The same study found more than once is kept once. 1,447 studies left. The step started from 4,520.

The 4,520 results were matched in three passes: the same DOI; then the same title with publication years no more than one apart; then a close title match, within the same year, for records with no DOI. Records with different DOIs were never merged. 3,073 duplicate results were merged, leaving 1,447 studies.
