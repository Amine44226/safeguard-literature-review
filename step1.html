Step 1. Search

All results from the seven sources. 4,520 results.

The seven sources were searched between 25 September and 4 October 2026 with four searches. Every search contains an addiction term ("digital addiction", "smartphone addiction", "smartphone dependence" or "problematic smartphone use") and the terms of one research question. Searches A, B and C were run separately on titles, abstracts and author keywords; search D on titles.

Results per source: Scopus 1,450; Web of Science 1,109; Europe PMC 950; PubMed 892; SpringerLink 79; IEEE Xplore 29; ACM 11.

Results per search: A, instruments (RQ1) 2,835; B, sensing (RQ2) 22; C, artificial intelligence (RQ4) 385; D, mental health and values (RQ3) 1,278.
