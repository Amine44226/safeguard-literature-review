Step 5. Title and abstract

Studies that fail filter layer 1 are removed. 694 studies left. The step started from 830.

The 830 titles and abstracts were screened against filter layer 1. A study was included if it:

  • studied behaviour or mental state through smartphones or similar mobile devices;
  • answered at least one of the review questions;
  • reported original work, such as a study, a system, a dataset or an analysis;
  • was published in 2015 or later, in English, in a peer-reviewed venue;
  • had a full text that could be obtained.

Studies that only mention the topic in passing, editorials, conference abstracts, posters, preprints, studies centred on genes or molecules and duplicates of a study already included were excluded. Each decision records the passage of the abstract it rests on. 136 studies were removed.
