Step 7. Full text and quality

Full texts are read and scored; studies that fail the criteria or score below 3.5 are removed. 47 studies left. The step started from 93.

The 91 full texts were read and checked against the eligibility criteria. 20 did not meet them: 12 did not meet the criteria of any review question; 3 used smartphone addiction only as a predictor of another outcome; 2 conference abstracts; 1 preprint; 1 not in English; 1 repeats the results of another study.

The studies were scored on six quality questions (aims, measures, data collection, methods, results shown, validity and reliability), each answered yes (1), partly (0.5) or no (0), following a written guide with rules for each study design. When two answers could apply to a question, the lower one was kept. Studies scoring 5 or more were rated high, 3.5 to 4.5 moderate and below 3.5 low. The selection rules and the quality cut-off were made final during full-text reading and scoring.

Of the 71 eligible studies, 24 scored below 3.5 and were removed. 47 studies are included: 5 of high quality (5 to 6) and 42 of moderate quality (3.5 to 4.5). Per question: RQ1 16, RQ2 5, RQ3 20, RQ4 11.

4 other full texts had been read before the step 5 decisions reported here were adopted (1 of them scored). These studies are excluded at step 5 and are not counted above. In all, 95 full texts were read and 82 studies were scored.

Rule in the protocol: section 5.7 and section 6 (quality).
