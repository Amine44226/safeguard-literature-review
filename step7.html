Step 7. Full text and quality

Full texts are read and scored; studies that fail the criteria or score below 3.5 are removed. 47 studies left. The step started from 93.

Full texts were sought for the 93 studies and 91 were obtained. Each was read in full and checked again against filter layer 1. Removed on reading: 6 answered none of the review questions; 2 conference abstracts; 1 preprint; 1 not in English; 1 repeats the results of another study.

The remaining 80 studies were scored on six quality questions (aims, measures, data collection, methods, results shown, validity and reliability), each answered yes (1), partly (0.5) or no (0), following a written guide with rules for each study design. When two answers could apply to a question, the lower one was kept. The cut-off was 3 when the studies were scored. It was raised to 3.5 on 4 November, after scoring.

9 scored studies were then removed under criteria amended after scoring: 6 failed filter layer 2 as amended on 8 and 22 November (step 6), and 3 were excluded on 22 November because smartphone addiction is only a predictor of another outcome in them, so they answer none of the review questions.

Of the 71 studies left, 24 scored below 3.5 and were removed (14 of them scored 3). 47 studies are included: 5 of high quality (5 to 6) and 42 of moderate quality (3.5 to 4.5). Per question: RQ1 16, RQ2 5, RQ3 20, RQ4 11.

4 other full texts had been read before the step 5 decisions reported here were adopted on 8 November (1 of them scored). These studies are excluded at step 5 and are not counted above. The study that repeats the results of another had also been scored before the repeat was found. In all, 95 full texts were read and 82 studies were scored.

Rule in the protocol: section 5.7 and section 6 (quality).
