Step 7. Full text and quality

Full texts are read and scored; studies below 3.5 are removed. 52 studies left. The step started from 89.

Full texts were sought for the 89 studies and 88 were obtained. Each was read in full and checked again against filter layer 1. Removed after reading: 6 answered none of the review questions; 2 conference abstracts; 1 preprint; 1 not in English; 1 repeats the results of another study.

The remaining 77 studies were scored on six quality questions (aims, measures, data collection, methods, results shown, validity and reliability), each answered yes (1), partly (0.5) or no (0), following a written guide with rules for each study design. When two answers could apply to a question, the lower one was kept.

25 studies scored below 3.5 and were removed. 52 studies are included: 5 of high quality (5 to 6) and 47 of moderate quality (3.5 to 4.5). Per question: RQ1 17, RQ2 5, RQ3 21, RQ4 14.

Rule in the protocol: section 5.7 and section 6 (quality).
